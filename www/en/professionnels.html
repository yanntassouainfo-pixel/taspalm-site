How it works, in four steps.

From the first message to delivery, four simple steps.

  1. I

    The need. You tell us the product, the use, the volume and the destination. We reply, with a sample if useful.

  2. II

    The quote. Volume, packaging, delivery terms. One page, not ten.

  3. III

    The order. Once the quote is accepted, the harvest or the lot is reserved for you.

  4. IV

    The delivery. By river then by road, to the destination agreed together.
