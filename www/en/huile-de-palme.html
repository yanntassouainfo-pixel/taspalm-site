From the tree to you.

Three moments.

I

The bunch

The bunches are cut when ripe, as the fruit turns red. They do not stay on the ground.

II

The pressing

Boiling, pressing, settling. The mill stands on the estate, right beside the palms.

III

The departure

Filled into jerrycans or drums, loaded into a pirogue or a truck. Lead times and destinations stated at order.
