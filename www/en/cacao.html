From the tree to you.

Three moments. The real photographs of each step are to be taken on site.

I

The pod

Picked ripe, opened the same day. The white pulp surrounds the beans.

II

The fermentation

The beans go into wooden boxes, covered with banana leaves. This is where the aroma is born.

III

The drying

On racks, turned several times a day, down to the target moisture. Then bagged.
