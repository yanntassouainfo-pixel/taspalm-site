From the tree to you.

Three moments. The real photographs of each step are to be taken on site.

I

The pod

The pods grow on the trunk. Picked ripe, they are opened the same day.

II

The fermentation

Out of the pod, the beans ferment for a few days. This is where the aroma is born.

III

The drying

On racks, turned several times a day, down to the target moisture. Then bagged.
