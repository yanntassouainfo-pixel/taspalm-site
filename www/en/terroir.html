A land between two rivers.

Ibenga, Enyellé district, Likouala department, in the north of the Republic of the Congo. Where the Ibenga meets the Ubangi.

Ibenga, Enyellé, Likouala.

The estate is bordered by the Ibenga river, near its confluence with the Ubangi, which marks the border with the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

It is reached by river or by road from Impfondo, the department's main town.
